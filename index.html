About me
Sobre mim

The person behind the platformDie Person hinter der PlattformA pessoa por trás da plataforma

I grew up in Brazil, built my career from junior engineer to Tech Lead at one of Brazil's largest industrial groups, and relocated to London in pursuit of bigger technical challenges and a broader stage. I hold an Italian passport alongside my Brazilian one which means I come with the ambition of someone who moved continents, and the stability of EU right to work.

Cresci no Brasil, construí minha carreira de engenheiro júnior a Tech Lead em um dos maiores grupos industriais do país, e me mudei para Londres em busca de desafios técnicos maiores e um palco mais amplo. Tenho passaporte italiano além do brasileiro o que significa que venho com a ambição de quem mudou de continente, e a estabilidade do direito de trabalho na UE.

I did two postgraduate degrees one in Data Science, one in Leadership & Technology Management because I've always believed the best data engineers understand the business as deeply as they understand the pipeline. That duality defines how I work: I write production code, and I present to the board. Sometimes on the same day.

Fiz duas pós-graduações uma em Ciência de Dados, outra em Liderança & Gestão de Tecnologia porque sempre acreditei que os melhores engenheiros de dados entendem o negócio tão profundamente quanto entendem o pipeline. Essa dualidade define como trabalho: escrevo código de produção e apresento para a diretoria. Às vezes no mesmo dia.

Pentaho → Databricks Cloud Migration
Migração Pentaho → Databricks
Problem
A legacy Pentaho ETL ecosystem could no longer support growing data volumes, complex SAP integrations, or the governance requirements of a scaling organisation. Pipelines were fragile, undocumented, and unversioned.
Solution
Architected a full migration to Databricks on Azure implementing Medallion Architecture across Bronze, Silver, and Gold layers, integrating 500+ tables from SAP HCM, S/4HANA, SharePoint, and 5 APIs, with full CI/CD governance and Unity Catalog for access control.
Impact
Zero data loss. Fully versioned, governed pipelines replaced brittle legacy jobs enabling the organisation to scale data products without engineering bottlenecks.
Problema
O ecossistema legado de ETL em Pentaho não conseguia mais suportar volumes crescentes de dados, integrações SAP complexas ou os requisitos de governança de uma organização em escala. Pipelines eram frágeis, sem documentação e sem versionamento.
Solução
Arquitetei a migração completa para Databricks no Azure implementando Medallion Architecture nas camadas Bronze, Silver e Gold, integrando 500+ tabelas de SAP HCM, S/4HANA, SharePoint e 5 APIs, com governança CI/CD e Unity Catalog para controle de acesso.
Impacto
Zero perda de dados. Pipelines totalmente versionados e governados substituíram jobs legados frágeis permitindo que a organização escalasse produtos de dados sem gargalos de engenharia.

Layer Reference

Medallion Architecture Layers

Each layer has a clear purpose, ownership, and quality standard. Data only moves forward when it meets the contract of the layer.

Azure Platform
  • ADF orchestrates all ingestion pipelines
  • Blob Storage as staging landing zone
  • Active Directory for identity governance
  • Key Vault for all secrets and credentials
  • DevOps for CI/CD, boards, and deployments
  • Asset Bundles for environment promotion
Bronze Ingestion
  • Raw data, exactly as received from source
  • No transformations or business logic
  • Full and incremental load patterns
  • Delta format with full history retention
  • Source of truth for all raw data
  • 300+ tables across all source systems
Silver Cleansing and Transformation
  • Validated, deduplicated, null-handled
  • Standardised types and naming
  • Cross-system joins and entity resolution
  • Domain-oriented datasets
  • 300+ conformed tables
  • Trusted single version of each entity
Gold Business Rules and Output
  • KPIs, aggregations, derived metrics
  • 478 structured views and data products
  • PII controls, RLS, and column masks
  • GDPR compliance enforcement
  • Outbound API transport connections
  • Feeds Power BI, Genie, Apps, and APIs
